ABOUT US

More Than a Meal.

The Copper Plate exists to give people in Lusaka a place where familiar flavours meet a fresh, modern setting — food worth gathering around, in a room worth staying in.

OUR STORY

How it started.

The Copper Plate began as a small idea — to create a welcoming place where people across Lusaka could enjoy good food and spend real time together, without the fuss of formal fine dining or the compromise of fast food. What started as a concept for a neighbourhood grill grew into a kitchen that takes Zambian flavours seriously, and a dining room built for conversation to run long.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

What we believe in.

Good food starts with good ingredients, thoughtful preparation, and people who genuinely care about the experience they're creating. We keep our kitchen simple and our standards high — every dish made to order, every plate meant to be shared.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need to reserve a table?+

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend reserving ahead for weekends and larger groups.

Do you offer takeaway?+

Yes. Order by phone, WhatsApp, or in person and we'll have it ready for pick-up.

Can I order through WhatsApp?+

Yes, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your order and pick-up time.

Do you cater for groups?+

Yes, we welcome groups of all sizes — just let us know ahead so we can prepare.

Do you have vegetarian options?+

Yes, several dishes across our menu are marked vegetarian.

Can I book the restaurant for a private event?+

Yes, get in touch and we'll help you plan it.

Do you accept mobile money?+

Yes, we accept major mobile money platforms along with cash and card.

Can I request a less spicy version of a dish?+

Of course — just let your server know when ordering.
